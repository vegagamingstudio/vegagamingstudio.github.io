Last updated: 25 November 2026

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not directly collect your name, email address or other personal details through the App.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device type, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is governed by Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the App asks for your consent or lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your data before personalised ads are shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

We do not store personal information on our own servers. Information collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.
