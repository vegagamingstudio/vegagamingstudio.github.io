Chess of Empires – Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026 · Last updated 25 November 2026 · Android app

Chess of Empires is a chess game made by Carlos Vega (Vega Gaming Studio), an independent developer in Sydney, Australia. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your games, settings and progress are saved only on your device. We never see them, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The free version of the game shows ads (banners on menu screens, a full-screen ad between games, and optional "watch an ad for a hint" ads). These are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • device type, operating system and app version
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps)

Google uses this to choose which ads to show, limit repeats, prevent fraud and report results. Google's handling of this data is covered by its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland). If you're in these regions, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy option in the game's settings menu.
  • US state privacy rights. Where applicable, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information through the same privacy option in the game's settings.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. On Android go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

Chess of Empires is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested in a way that limits personalisation where required by law.

Data security and retention

Because game data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security. We do not store personal information on our own servers. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
